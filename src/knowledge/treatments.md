# City Health Clinic - Treatments

## Preventive Care

- Annual physical examinations
- Health risk assessments
- Immunizations and vaccines
- Cancer screenings (mammogram, colonoscopy referrals)
- Wellness coaching

## Diagnostic Services

- Laboratory blood work and analysis
- Digital X-ray imaging
- Electrocardiogram (ECG)
- Vision and hearing testing
- Skin biopsies

## Therapeutic Services

- Physiotherapy and rehabilitation
- Nutritional therapy and diet planning
- Pain management programs
- Stress management and relaxation techniques
- Post-surgical recovery care

## Specialized Medical Care

- Cardiology consultations and heart health monitoring
- Dermatology treatments for skin conditions
- Pediatric care for infants, children, and adolescents
- ENT treatments for ear, nose, and throat conditions
- Dental care and oral health services

## Chronic Disease Management

- Diabetes monitoring and management
- Hypertension (high blood pressure) control
- Asthma treatment and pulmonary function testing
- Cholesterol management
- Arthritis pain management

## Emergency and Urgent Care

- Walk-in urgent care for non-life-threatening emergencies
- First aid treatment
- Minor wound care and stitching
- Fever and infection treatment
- Immediate care for sprains and strains
