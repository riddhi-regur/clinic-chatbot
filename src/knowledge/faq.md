# City Health Clinic - Frequently Asked Questions

## Clinic Hours

- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM
- Public Holidays: Closed (emergency line available)

## Location and Parking

We are located at 123 Health Avenue, Medical District.
Free parking is available with 100 spaces in our lot.
Handicap-accessible parking is available near the main entrance.

## Insurance

We accept most major insurance plans including:

- BlueCross BlueShield
- Aetna
- UnitedHealthcare
- Cigna
- Medicare
  Please bring your insurance card to every visit.

## Appointments

- Walk-ins are welcome for urgent care
- Recommended to book appointments for regular checkups
- Online booking available through this chatbot
- Phone booking: 555-0100

## Cancellation Policy

- Please provide at least 24 hours notice for cancellations
- Late cancellations may incur a small fee
- No-shows will be noted in your patient record

## Lab Results

- Routine results: 24-48 hours
- Specialized tests: 3-5 business days
- Results available through patient portal or phone

## Emergency Contact

For medical emergencies, call 911.
For after-hours clinic concerns: 555-0199
This is not a substitute for emergency services.

## Doctors

We have 12 specialist doctors on staff. Each doctor specializes in specific services. You can ask about any service to find the right doctor for your needs.

## What to Bring to Your Visit

- Insurance card
- Photo ID
- List of current medications
- Previous medical records (if applicable)
- Payment method for co-pay
